## Description
The world has been reset. No houses, no roads, no tools, not even a knife. Zero has half a day, a thirsty body and a wild valley he knows nothing about. Before food, fire or a house, he has to answer one question: where is it safe?

In this episode Zero finds east and west with a shadow stick, reads the valley from high ground, asks every direction three questions (What is useful? What can hurt me? Which way do I run?), and learns why the most convenient campsite can be the most dangerous one.

Unlocked: a survey map and the first base site.
Next episode: the river looks crystal clear. Is it safe to drink?

This is a cartoon for learning. The survival numbers are rough guides, not hard rules.
