# EP 01.03 — From an Ordinary Stone to Sharp Stone Flakes (beat outline, ~10:00)

Mission 01 · Survive From Zero. Single question: "How does a plain stone become something that cuts?" Tangible result: a few usable flakes (sharp edges) on the base site.

World state coming in (after 01.02): dirt map with base-site stick marker and water mark, shadow-direction pole + 2 stones, a torn leaf cup (ragged edge, leaks). No fire, no tools. Everything on screen is natural; explanations are holograms in the LAB.

Concept-level only. No step-by-step knapping instructions.

## Beats

1. **0:00–0:30 · Cold open** — Base site, dawn. Zero tries to tear a thick vine/hide-like plant stem with bare hands; it stretches and will not part. Cut to the torn leaf cup, ragged and leaking. Hook line: "Everything I make tears. I need an edge." LAB: none (a visual question mark on the ragged leaf edge).

2. **0:30–0:50 · Cold open to goal** — Zero looks at the river glint far off, then at a stone in hand: smooth, round, blunt. Beat: "Rock is the hardest thing here. Why can't it cut?" LAB: none, mission HUD flips on.

3. **0:50–1:15 · Goal and conditions** — Base site, map on the ground. Goal card: "Get a cutting edge from stone, using only what the valley gives." Conditions: only hands and other stones, no fire, daylight limited, injury cannot be treated. LAB: none.

4. **1:15–1:50 · Inspect: the gravel bar** — River gravel bar by the water mark. Zero lays out round pebbles, a pale grey-brown glassy nodule, a speckled granite cobble. Rolls each in hand, compares weight, grain, and sheen. LAB: hologram labels "grain vs. glassy" (visual compare only).

5. **1:50–2:30 · Inspect: red rocks outcrop** — Red rock face above the bar. Zero finds broken fragments at the foot with curved, shell-like scars where natural rockfall cracked them. Collects a handful of candidates. Insight: nature already showed the trick. LAB: none (sets up the next section).

6. **2:30–3:15 · Principle (LAB), part 1: why round stones do not cut** — LAB hologram cross-section of a river cobble: edges rounded by water wear, many tiny blunt contact points. Zero's voice: a cutting edge needs a very thin meeting of two surfaces. LAB idea: edge angle and why thin means sharp.

7. **3:15–4:00 · Principle (LAB), part 2: conchoidal fracture** — Hologram of force traveling through a fine-grained glassy stone as a cone, peeling off a curved flake with a smooth shell-like surface (like a thick glass shard). LAB idea: stress waves spread in a cone from the impact point; a uniform, grain-free material breaks along that cone, leaving a very thin edge.

8. **4:00–4:30 · Principle (LAB), part 3: flint vs. granite** — Split-screen hologram. Flint/chert: tiny uniform crystals, no preferred cleavage, predictable curved break. Granite: coarse mixed crystals (quartz, feldspar, mica) that fracture along grain boundaries, giving crumbly, uneven pieces. LAB idea: "the stone's inside decides how it breaks." Mini ranking card of candidates.

9. **4:30–5:20 · Experiment 1: a quick test of candidates** — Gravel bar. Zero (at a safe distance, flat sandy spot, rocks set on a cloth-like mat of leaves) does a simple "which stone breaks cleanly" check by striking one stone against another once or twice. Shows the glassy nodule sheds a thin curved piece while granite crumbles or dents. LAB: result cards pinned to the map ("breaks clean / crumbles"). No technique detail shown or narrated.

10. **5:20–6:00 · Experiment 2: first usable flake** — Same spot. Zero holds up a thin flake against sunlight; the edge looks translucent and straight. Tests on a plant stem from the base site: it parts cleanly. Small win beat, brief music lift. LAB: hologram zoom of the edge, thinner than a leaf's edge.

11. **6:00–6:30 · Safety caveat (on screen, in character)** — Zero pauses to note flying chips: wears a leaf-cloth wrap on the eyes in a cartoon-safe way, keeps hands behind the blow, picks up fragments only after the work is done. LAB idea: tiny shards travel fast and are invisible to the eye, so protect eyes and cover cuts; this is an explanation of risk, not a how-to. A caption states that real stone chipping needs eye protection, gloves, and an experienced teacher.

12. **6:30–7:10 · Reasoned failure** — Zero tries to use the thin flake in bare fingers for a harder task (cutting a thick green branch / scraping bark). The edge chips, bites into the hand, and Zero drops it, flinching. Slow-mo on the chipped edge.

13. **7:10–7:45 · Analysis of the failure** — LAB hologram reasons it out: a very sharp edge is also very thin and brittle; force concentrated in a tiny area breaks the edge, and an unprotected grip puts the hand at risk. LAB idea: sharpness vs. strength trade-off (edge angle again, tying back to beat 6). Zero lists the causes on the map.

14. **7:45–8:30 · Fix 1: protect the hand, strengthen the use** — Base site. Zero keeps a thicker, blunt back on the flake and holds it by the thick side, wrapping the grip with a folded leaf pad; uses lighter, controlled pressure suited to the job. LAB idea: pick the right edge angle for the task (thin for slicing soft, sturdier for scraping).

15. **8:30–9:15 · Test of success** — Zero re-cuts the torn leaf cup edge into a neat, even shape and trims the ragged vine strand cleanly. Side-by-side with the beat-1 tear. Result: a clean cut, a neater cup rim (still leaky, still no fire or containers). LAB: before/after hologram of the edge.

16. **9:15–9:40 · Summary and knowledge cards** — Cards: (1) round stones are blunt, (2) fine-grained glassy stone fractures in curved cones and leaves thin edges, (3) granite crumbles, (4) thin edge = sharp but brittle, (5) protect eyes and hands. Short LAB recap hologram.

17. **9:40–9:55 · Unlock and checklist** — Card `UNLOCKED: stone flakes (cutting edge)`. World-state update: flakes stored at the base site, map gains a "stone source: gravel bar / red rocks" mark. No UNLOCKED EARLIER cards needed beyond the map and leaf cup.

18. **9:55–10:00 · Next-episode hook (to 01.04)** — Evening sky darkens, wind picks up, clouds over the ridge. Zero looks at the open base site with no cover: "Edge in hand. Now where do I sleep when the rain comes?" Card: NEXT — 01.04 Temporary Shelter: how to block sun and rain.
