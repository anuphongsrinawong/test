## Description
Day two, and Zero still has not had a drop to drink. There is no fire, no pot and no container yet, so he cannot boil water. All he can do is choose the source as smartly as possible.

Zero folds a leaf into a cup and tests five kinds of water in his valley with four simple questions: Is it flowing or still? What is upstream? Are there signs of animals? What about color, smell and anything floating in it? The clearest water of all turns out to fail the hardest, because the most dangerous things in water are the ones your eyes cannot see.

Unlocked: a water source map and four questions for choosing water.
Next episode: a world with nothing but rocks. Where do you find something sharp?

SAFETY: this is a cartoon story, not a survival guide. In real life, always boil natural water or treat it with a reliable method before you drink it. Clear water, spring water and groundwater can all still carry germs.
